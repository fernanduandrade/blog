After shipping a few projects over the last years, I wanted to write down what I've learned about building, shipping and maintaining a SaaS as a solo developer.

## 1. Start small, validate fast

The best ideas usually look boring at the beginning. I try to build the smallest version possible and put it in front of real users as early as I can.

> You don't need more features. You need more signal.

## 2. Ship and iterate

Shipping is a skill. The more you ship, the better you get. I focus on shipping small improvements consistently instead of big infrequent launches.

## 3. Keep costs and complexity low

As a solo developer, every extra tool, service or integration adds cognitive load. I constantly ask myself:

- Do I really need this?
- What happens if this service goes down?
- Can I build a simpler version?

## 4. Focus on retention

Acquisition is sexy, retention is where the money is. From day one, I measure retention before anything else.

## 5. Solve real problems

Sounds obvious, but it's easy to get distracted building features nobody asked for. I spend more time talking to users than building.
