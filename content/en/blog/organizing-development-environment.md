After years of tweaking and refining, I've arrived at a setup that makes me genuinely more productive. Here's what I use.

## Terminal

I use **iTerm2** with the Catppuccin Mocha theme. The pane splitting and profiles configuration is what I use most.

For the shell, I use **zsh** with a minimal configuration file — no heavy frameworks like Oh My Zsh.

## Editor

**VS Code** with minimal extensions:

- GitLens
- Prettier
- ESLint
- Vim (yes, inside VS Code)

## Project management

I use **Raycast** for everything: opening projects, searching files, calculating, converting. It's the biggest time-saver in my day.

## Dotfiles

My entire setup is versioned in a public repository. If you want to see the full configuration, check the link in the projects page.

## The most important thing

The best setup is the one you don't think about. Optimize to the point where the tools disappear and the work comes first.
